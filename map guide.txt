The isSecret column is the last one. A secret room stays off the map the player sees until the player
walks into it, so its flag is the only thing that keeps it hidden - the name does not.

Stats are strength, wisdom, agility or health, in lower case. Flags are true, false or empty.
Blank lines are skipped. The map is refused if no exits lead from room 0 to a win room, if room 0
has enemies, if a line has a column past isSecret, or if a room an exit leads to can never be entered:
its condition needs more of a stat, or one of its enemies deals at least as much damage as the player
has health, even with the starting 10 plus every item from the rooms the player can enter before it.
A condition is checked only the first time a room is entered.

Example:
1,Castle Entrance,2,-1,8,0,A nice room, full of nice things.,Shield:health:3;Cursed Amulet:health:-10,Skeleton:15:3:20;Bat:6:8:30,The ground rumbles...,Sword:10:strength:5;Bow:12:agility:10,strength:10:You need to be strong to pry the door;wisdom:5:Looks like you don't know how to handle a crowbar,true,You win!!!,false
